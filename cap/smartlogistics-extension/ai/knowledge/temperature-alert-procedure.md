# SmartLogistics Temperature Alert Procedure

## Purpose

This procedure defines how a logistics operator should respond when a shipment reports a temperature alert.

## Scope

The procedure applies to shipments carrying temperature-sensitive goods or equipment when a temperature threshold has been exceeded or when a temperature-related incident has been created.

## Initial Assessment

When a temperature alert is detected, the operator must:

1. Confirm the current shipment status.
2. Review the incident severity and incident description.
3. Contact the carrier to confirm whether the temperature excursion is still active.
4. Determine how long the shipment has remained outside the permitted temperature range.
5. Verify whether the shipment contains temperature-sensitive cargo.

## Severity Guidelines

### LOW

A minor temperature deviation occurred for a short period and there is no evidence of cargo damage.

Recommended response:
- Continue monitoring.
- Record the deviation.
- Confirm that temperature has returned to the acceptable range.

### MEDIUM

The temperature threshold has been exceeded and the duration or cargo impact is not yet known.

Recommended response:
- Contact the carrier immediately.
- Request the latest temperature reading.
- Place the shipment under enhanced monitoring.
- Keep the incident in IN_PROGRESS status until the temperature is confirmed as stable.

### HIGH

A significant or prolonged temperature excursion has occurred and cargo quality may be affected.

Recommended response:
- Escalate the incident to the logistics supervisor.
- Contact the carrier immediately.
- Request temperature history for the shipment.
- Notify the receiving location that delivery may require inspection.
- Do not close the incident until cargo condition has been assessed.

### CRITICAL

There is evidence that the cargo is damaged, unsafe, or no longer compliant with required transport conditions.

Recommended response:
- Escalate immediately to the logistics supervisor and responsible quality team.
- Stop normal delivery processing where operationally possible.
- Inform the receiving location.
- Preserve temperature records and carrier evidence.
- Follow the applicable cargo rejection or quarantine procedure.

## Incident Resolution

A temperature incident may be resolved only after:

- the shipment temperature is stable or the shipment has completed delivery;
- the operational impact has been assessed;
- any required quality inspection has been completed;
- the final action has been documented.

## Escalation Rule

A MEDIUM temperature incident should be escalated to HIGH if:

- the excursion continues for more than 2 hours;
- the carrier cannot confirm the current temperature;
- the shipment contains sensitive cargo and cargo integrity cannot be confirmed.

A HIGH incident should be escalated to CRITICAL if there is evidence that the cargo is damaged, unsafe, or unusable.

## Operator Checklist

Before resolving a temperature alert, confirm:

- Current shipment status
- Latest temperature reading
- Duration of temperature excursion
- Carrier confirmation
- Cargo impact assessment
- Required escalation completed
- Final action documented
